Music is life

While I consider myself a composer, most people know me as a pianist and keyboard player. I have frequently performed as a musician for live shows and as a solo artist rocking out piano bar performances.

I studied composition at the University of New England under Ann Ghandar, and I enjoy bringing pieces of different styles together.

I play piano, guitar, bass, saxophone, flute and a few others. My style is mostly pop-rock piano, with Stevie Wonder, Ben Folds, The Darkness, Hoodoo Gurus and Jacob Collier as my main influences.
